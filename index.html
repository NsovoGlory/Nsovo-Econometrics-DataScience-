Forecasting South African Inflation: SARIMA & VAR Econometric Analysis

End-to-End Inflation Forecasting System: Univariate & Multivariate Time Series Modeling

Built an end-to-end inflation forecasting system for South Africa using two complementary econometric approaches: SARIMA for univariate time series forecasting and VAR for multivariate macroeconomic analysis. Identified seasonal persistence (56.6% combined seasonal AR), monetary policy transmission lag (4 months), and oil price sensitivity (17% of forecast variance) as key drivers of South African inflation dynamics. The system provides actionable insights for monetary policy decision-making and short-term inflation monitoring.

Commodity Prices and the South African Rand

An Econometric and Machine Learning Analysis of the Rand’s Relationship with Global Commodity Markets

Investigated how global commodity prices transmit into the South African rand using cointegration, VECM, GARCH, and machine learning on 19 years of monthly data. Found that platinum not gold or oil has a statistically confirmed long run relationship with the rand, with a 1% platinum price rise linked to ~1.22% rand appreciation and ~6.8% monthly equilibrium correction. GARCH modelling showed persistent rand volatility (half life ~5.4 months) but no evidence of crisis amplified spillover from platinum. Machine learning (XGBoost + SHAP) revealed gold and Brent, despite lacking a long run relationship, carry the strongest short term predictive signal distinguishing structural relevance from forecasting value. Core findings were independently corroborated by peer reviewed literature on the same question.

Skills: R | Cointegration | VECM | GARCH | XGBoost | SHAP | Time Series Analysis | Financial Econometrics
